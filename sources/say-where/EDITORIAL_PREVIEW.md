### Gajah sama gajah berjuang, pelanduk mati di tengah-tengah.
- Language: Malay
- Literal image: Elephants fight each other; the mouse-deer dies between them
- Sense: Ordinary people suffer when powerful rivals clash
- Source: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=82736&keyword=gajah

### Mapenzi ni kikohozi hayawezi kufichika
