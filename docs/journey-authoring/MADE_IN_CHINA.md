## Editorial and visual decisions

- Short setup followed by one task; explanations and documentary captions belong after commitment.
- Real map relationships do the work. No exclusive wheat/rice border, fabricated river, decorative fort route or invented resource boundary.
- The fort’s modern relief image is a clue, never grading geometry. Comparison sites are explicitly qualified in Sources.
- Porcelain’s map marks an already known place, rather than treating it as an answer target for an abstract materials choice.
- Shanghai illustrates the role of a port; it does not assert a single historical porcelain export itinerary.
- Shenzhen’s transformation is the final reveal. The 1982 and 2025 photographs use different viewpoints and do not imply that no city existed before 1980.
- Completion keeps five places, clears question-specific terrain and rivers, and connects resources, movement and human decisions. Answer review retains actual mistakes from the current edition.
