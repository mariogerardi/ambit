# The Sound of Somewhere: Reggaetón

Unlisted editorial revision 3, 19 October 2026.
Practice route: `/journey/the-sound-of-somewhere-reggaeton`.

The story begins with Puerto Rico's underground, traces its roots through
Caribbean migration and Spanish-language reggae, follows a collaborative
recording to New York, examines mainland–island circulation, and ends with
Colombia's contribution to reggaetón's expanding pop audience. These are connected
chapters of the genre's evolution, without claiming one exclusive birthplace or
one linear itinerary.

## Editorial and source decisions

- Musical context is supplied in narration, with each reveal developing the next
  connection. The former Jamaica giveaway, allegations quiz, hidden Medellín
  estimate and implementation commentary in the New York reveal are removed.
- [Smithsonian Folkways](https://slides.com/smithsonianfolkways/music-of-puerto-rico-09-notes/fullscreen)
  supports migration and reggae en español.
  [Wayne Marshall](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2013/07/dembow-a-loop-history/)
  supports collaborative recording history, mixtapes and international breakout.
- [Raquel Z. Rivera's 1996 account](https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/atc/2425.html)
  supports the 1995 seizures as reveal context; allegations are no longer a task.
- [USAGov](https://www.usa.gov/visit-territories), the
  [Library of Congress Puerto Rico guide](https://guides.loc.gov/puerto-rico-research-guide)
  and its [migration history](https://www.loc.gov/classroom-materials/immigration/puerto-rican-cuban/migrating-to-a-new-land/)
  support the territory, citizenship and diaspora context. The question asks
  political status, not travel-document or immigration advice.
- [El Colombiano](https://www.elcolombiano.com/cultura/historia-del-regatonen-medellin-jbalvin-karol-g-maluma-CM21696037),
  [Nicky Jam's account](https://www.univision.com/musica/reggaeton/nicky-jam-hubiera-terminado-en-cenizas)
  and the [Washington Post timeline](https://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/interactive/2024/reggaeton-dembow-evolution-timeline/)
  support the Colombian musical chapter. The relocation year remains omitted
  because commonly cited 2007 and a later artist account giving 2008 differ.
- San Juan and Medellín are geographic endpoints for a great-circle comparison,
  not a surveyed flight itinerary. Freeport is a reviewed town point, not an
  invented HC&F building coordinate. Provenance details stay in author notes.
