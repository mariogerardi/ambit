# Where Is It Official? — sources and acceptance policy

Reviewed November 3, 2026. This is an unregistered source-workbench draft. Source
attestation is distinct from final native-speaker editorial review and from real
browser verification of shaping, wrapping and small-country interaction.

## Scope

One nationally qualifying country is sufficient. Co-official status and expressly
prescribed official government use count. Accepted geography is independent of
map coloring, camera framing and assistance. Regional-only recognition and mere
speaker populations do not establish acceptance. The supported `wurl:PSE` map unit
is selectable even though the ordinary UN-member roster omits it. This is a map
unit policy, not a new political classification.

The approved generous policy accepts Hassaniya Arabic in Mali and the Indonesian
national standard in the Malay round. The displayed Arabic expression is Standard
Arabic; the Malay proverb is attested in Malay. Both distinctions are explained
on reveal. Indonesia's official designation remains Bahasa Indonesia.

- [Chad presidency: 2023 Constitution](https://presidencetchad.org/tchad/constitution/) and [Ministry of Education](https://e-education.td/): French and Arabic.
- [Comoros Ministry of Justice: 2014 law on Arabic and French in state acts](https://justice.gouv.km/texte/loi-du-26-juin-2014-portant-utilisation-de-larabe-et-du-francais-langues-officielles-dans-les-ecrits-et-les-actes-de-letat/).

- [Iraqi National Investment Commission: Constitution, Article 4](https://investpromo.gov.iq/policies-and-laws/iraqi-constitution/): Arabic and Kurdish.
- [Lebanon Constitutional Council: fundamental provisions, Article 11](https://cc.gov.lb/en/constitution-and-laws/constitution/fundamental-provisions/).
- France Diplomatie current country profiles corroborate [Libya](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/information-par-pays/libye/presentation-de-la-libye), [Mauritania](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/information-par-pays/mauritanie/presentation-de-la-mauritanie), and [Yemen](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/information-par-pays/yemen/presentation-du-yemen).

- [Somalia Constitution, Article 5, WIPO-hosted primary text](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/text/498470): Somali is named the official language and Arabic the second language. Its constitutionally recognized national status is included under the approved scope; the two wordings are not identical.
- [Sudan embassy: country information](https://www.sudanembassy.org.uk/about-sudan/) identifies Arabic as official. Older constitutional descriptions of official working languages must not be mistaken for a uniform constitutional arrangement across the network.
- [Syria: 2025 Constitutional Declaration, UNHCR Refworld](https://www.refworld.org/legal/legislation/natlegbod/2025/ar/150231). Use the transitional declaration, not an obsolete pre-transition constitution.
- [United Arab Emirates Constitution, Article 7](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1000/download).
- [Palestinian official-language provision, WAFA](https://info.wafa.ps/Pages/Details/29604): amended Basic Law Article 4(3).
- [Mali Constitution, Article 31, WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/22754): national languages are official. [UK PCGN Mali factfile](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/1186430/Mali_toponymic_factfile-updated_2018.pdf) explicitly lists Hassaniya Arabic. Despite the old filename, the accessed PDF carries Crown Copyright 2025 and discusses the 2023 change.
- The supplied [Nations Online language page](https://www.nationsonline.org/oneworld/countries_by_languages.htm) is a secondary cross-check; its broader spoken-language lists are not themselves the official-status answer contract. In particular, speaker populations in Eritrea, Israel or South Sudan do not add those countries to this round.
- Proverb corroboration: [Birzeit University's Qabas dictionary](https://sina.birzeit.edu/qabas/lemma/2020148100), and the Arabic/English pair in [American River College's 2025 Parrot newsletter, issue 191](https://arc.losrios.edu/arc/shared/doc/instruction/humanities/ARC-English-Second-Language/ARC-Parrot-Newsletter/ARC-Parrot-Archives/2025/The-Parrot-Issue-191-Spring-2025.pdf). ## Portuguese

- [Turismo de Portugal: CPLP](https://www.turismodeportugal.pt/en/quem_somos/cooperacao-internacional/Pages/cplp-comunidade-dos-paises-de-lingua-portuguesa.aspx), updated April 2026, lists the nine members. [CPLP member states](https://www.cplp.org/estados-membros/) provides the organization's own reference.
- [Timor-Leste Ministry of Education](https://moe.gov.tl/en/national-curriculum/) identifies Tetum and Portuguese as official.
- Equatorial Guinea's 2010 adoption is documented by [its government](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_portugues_sera_el_tercer_idioma_oficial_de_la_republica_de_guinea_ecuatorial) and [RTP/Lusa's report of the promulgated decree](https://www.rtp.pt/noticias/mundo/obiang-promulga-decreto-que-estabelece-portugues-como-terceira-lingua-oficial-da-guine-equatorial_n361490). Legal status qualifies independently of everyday fluency.
- [CPT's contemporary Portuguese article](https://www.cpt.com.br/dicas-cursos-cpt/5-dicas-para-evitar-impasses-nas-negociacoes) attests the expression as the opening of a longer sentence ending “para encontrar soluções.” The clue uses that self-contained opening and adds a period. It is not presented as a historical proverb.
- Macau's regional official status falls outside this country-level set.

## Russian

- [Belarus Constitution, Article 17, ILO-hosted primary document](https://wwwex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/47034/BLR-47034.pdf): Belarusian and Russian.
- [Kyrgyz Republic Constitution, Article 13](https://www.gov.kg/ru/p/constitution): Russian is used as an official language.
- [Kazakhstan government: 2026 Constitution, Article 9](https://www.gov.kz/org/maslikhat-ulken-naryn/press/news/details/1187713?lang=en): Kazakh is named the official language; Russian is officially used alongside it in state and local institutions. This is the approved official-government-use qualification.
- [Repeat article](https://www.repeat.ru/article/kak-nachat-zanimatsya-sportom-i-sdelat-ehto-privychkoj) attests the expression with е and a question mark. The clue explicitly writes ё in всё and uses a period; its meaning is unchanged.

## Malay and Indonesian

- [Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka search](https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=112548&keyword=bagai+aur+dengan+tebing) and [DBP's literature article](https://tunascipta.jendeladbp.my/2021/10/23/1712/) attest the full bamboo/riverbank proverb. The earlier `d=82736` search returned a gateway error; the indexed DBP records corroborate the wording.
- [Malaysia's government](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/government/kenali-malaysia/official-language): Article 152 and the National Language Act.
- [Brunei Information Department](https://www.information.gov.bn/Pages/AboutBrunei.aspx) and [Brunei's UN mission](https://www.un.int/brunei/brunei/country-facts): Malay is official.
- [Singapore Constitution, Article 153A](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CONS1963?Phrase=Official+Languages&ProvIds=pr153A-&ViewType=Within&WiAl=1): Malay, Mandarin, Tamil and English. Direct access returned 403, but the official indexed text exposed this provision.
- [Indonesia's language agency](https://badanbahasa.kemendikdasmen.go.id/artikel-detail/854/bahasa-sebagai-pemersatu-bangsa) explains Indonesian's development from Malay. Indonesia qualifies under the approved inclusive-standard rule. The clue is not claimed to be the standard Indonesian wording.

## Presentation and attribution

All five clues are selectable Unicode text, not images. Arabic uses an RTL text
block with an explicit language tag; captions remain English/LTR. Translation and
language-name nodes are inserted only after commitment. Fonts use the shared UI
stack with system script fallback; glyph coverage and small-screen shaping still
benefit from player/native-speaker review.

Whole-network coloring is explanatory blue. The actual submitted country retains
green/rose judgment. Recap promotes the accepted selection, or one explicit
illustrative answer after a miss, and preserves actual mistakes. Display geometry
is the existing Natural Earth globe; no country polygons were enlarged or
regenerated. Existing geographic attribution applies. No outside image or audio
assets were introduced.
